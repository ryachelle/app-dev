# 📺 Sex and the City

---

## 📝 Overview

**Sex and the City** is an **American romantic comedy-drama television series** created by **Darren Star** and produced by *HBO*.  
It originally aired from **June 6, 1998** to **February 22, 2004**.

- **Genre:** Comedy, Drama, Romance   
- **Based on:** *Sex and the City* by Candace Bushnell  
- **Seasons:** 6  
- **Episodes:** 94  

---

## 🌆 Main Characters

| Character | Actress | Description |
|------------|----------|--------------|
| Carrie Bradshaw | Sarah Jessica Parker | A writer and columnist who explores love and relationships in New York City. |
| Charlotte York | Kristin Davis | A romantic art dealer with traditional values. |
| Miranda Hobbes | Cynthia Nixon | A career-focused lawyer who values independence. |
| Samantha Jones | Kim Cattrall | A confident PR executive with a bold approach to love and sex. |

---

## 💬 Themes

1. **Friendship** – The bond between the four women is the heart of the show.  
2. **Love & Relationships** – Explores dating, marriage, and breakups.  
3. **Sexual Freedom** – Celebrates openness and empowerment.  
4. **City Life** – A love letter to **New York City**, almost like a fifth character.
